## Camera recon (read-only, no I2C traffic)

- ACPI already enumerates the sensors as I2C clients: `i2c-NVDA2810:00` on i2c-0 (\_SB.CAMF, OV08X40)
  and `i2c-NVDA2812:00` on i2c-4 (\_SB.CAMI, VD55G0); status 15, no driver bound. So no
  new_device needed — only a match.
- `ov08x40.ko` is in the -yoga tree but matches only `acpi:OVTI08F4` / `of:ovti,ov08x40` (no i2c id
  table). First patch: add NVDA2810 to its ACPI table, plus software-node properties
  (clock-frequency, endpoint/link-frequencies) and power sequencing for GPIO 0x22 (campower)
  / 0x27 (reset) on gpiochip0 (NVDA9221, pinctrl_paris) — currently unclaimed.
- SENI (NVDA2820) and ISP1 (NVDA2801) are present as platform devices, status 15; GCE0 has no _STA.
- Needed: ov08x40.c from the NV-Kernels 7.0 tree (not in the headers) to build out of tree.
